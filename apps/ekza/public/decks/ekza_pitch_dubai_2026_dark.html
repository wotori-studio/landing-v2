06Revenue sharing

Revenue follows the contribution graph.

Each sale, rental or license can route value back to the contributors attached to that asset.

Programmable splits

Every transaction can trigger automatic payouts to the full contributor graph — no invoices, no chasing.

The blockchain is the settlement rail, not the pitch. The product is trusted collaboration and repeatable licensing. The model is rail-agnostic — the same graph can settle over conventional payments and ordinary contracts; Solana is simply the first rail.
